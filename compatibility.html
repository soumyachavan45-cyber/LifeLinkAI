O-

Universal Red Cell Donor (O-)

CRITICAL IN TRAUMA

O- negative red blood cells have no A, B, or Rh antigens on their surface. This means anyone of any blood group can receive O- blood without immediate risk of immune rejection. It is vital in ambulances and trauma ERs.

AB+

Universal Recipient (AB+)

UNIVERSAL RECEIVER

AB+ individuals have both A, B, and Rh antigens present on their red cells. Therefore, their immune plasma does not carry anti-A, anti-B, or anti-Rh antibodies, allowing them to safely receive red blood cells from any blood group!
