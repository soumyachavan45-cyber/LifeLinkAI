FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know

Clear answers to common questions regarding voluntary blood donation and emergency safety.

❓ How often can I donate blood?

Whole blood can be safely donated every 56 days (8 weeks). Platelet donations can be made every 7 days (up to 24 times per year), while plasma can be donated every 28 days.

❓ Will donating blood weaken my immune system?

No! The average adult body contains 10-12 pints of blood and donates only 1 pint (about 450ml). Your body replaces the fluid plasma within 24 hours and red blood cells within 4-6 weeks.

❓ What is the Universal Blood Donor type?

O Negative (O-) is the universal red blood cell donor because its red blood cells lack A, B, and Rh antigens, making it safe for any emergency patient regardless of their blood group.
