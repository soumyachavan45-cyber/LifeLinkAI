CLINICAL FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, medical answers to common questions regarding voluntary blood donation and emergency safety.

❓ How often can an eligible adult donate blood?

Whole blood can be safely donated every 56 days (8 weeks) for healthy individuals. Platelet donations can be made every 7 days (up to 24 times per year), while plasma can be donated every 28 days.

❓ Will donating blood weaken my immune system or energy?

No! The average adult body contains 10-12 pints of blood and donates only 1 pint (about 450ml). Your body replaces the fluid plasma within 24 hours, and red blood cells are fully replenished in 4-6 weeks.

❓ Why is O Negative (O-) called the Universal Donor?

O Negative (O-) red blood cells lack A, B, and Rh antigens, meaning any patient's immune system will accept them without adverse reactions. In trauma surgeries where blood typing cannot wait, O- is life-saving.

❓ How is patient and donor personal information protected?

LifeLink complies with HIPAA and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. All medical notes and phone numbers are encrypted with 256-bit SSL, and emergency dispatches are only routed to certified healthcare centers.
